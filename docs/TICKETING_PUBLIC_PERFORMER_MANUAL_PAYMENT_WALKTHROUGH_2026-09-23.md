# Ticketing owner walkthrough — public performer and manual payment

Local code review and offline audits passed on 23 September 2026. The owner should verify these UI and email flows in their own browser; no local browser was launched during implementation.

1. Open `/tickets` on a public sale day. Under **Public Buyer → Watch a Performance**, search a solo winner and an ensemble member. Check the displayed competition venue/date/time, enter a different buyer's contact, and buy five Presto tickets. The review should show five complimentary orchestra places and no performer allowance. After verified payment, Orchestra Assignments should show five public places awaiting a session; assign and check the buyer email names the selected performance's orchestra venue/date/time.
2. For the same three-member ensemble, buy two tickets through **Registered Winner**. After payment, the shared group should show **5 public + 2 winner + 3 performers = 10**. Re-save the group's orchestra assignment and confirm both booking buyers receive the updated group details. Repeating an identical save should not consume ten places again.
3. Under **Public Buyer → Orchestra Ticket**, choose a session from the dropdown. Confirm Presto/Allegro free seating, purchased quantity only, and no second complimentary orchestra entitlement.
4. Repeat a competition purchase with **Use international payment options (PayNow or bank transfer)** checked. The button should create a booking acknowledgement instead of opening Paper.id. Check the email for ticket amount in IDR, booking ID, PayNow/bank details and payment-proof instructions. The booking remains pending with reserved inventory after 30 minutes. In Public Customers, verify **Send/Resend Payment Info**, then mark paid only after confirming payment. Check seat ownership, confirmation email and assignment demand.
5. Make a separate manual booking without paying. In Public Customers, verify no payment was received and choose **Cancel Unpaid**. The booking should become terminal and release its complete competition seat/capacity reservation. A paid booking or a pending Paper.id booking without a known invoice must not use this action.
6. Retry a manual checkout with the same key/cart after an instruction-email failure; it should reuse the same booking ID and retry delivery. Change the payment option or cart and confirm it cannot reuse that checkout key silently. Check both English and Indonesian labels and mobile layout manually.
